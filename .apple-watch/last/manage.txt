	(function () {
		// Prevent React dev mode from running this multiple times
		if (window.__didRunColorThemeScript) {
			return;
		}
		if (window.angular) {
			return;
		}
		window.__didRunColorThemeScript = true;
		const prefersDark = window.matchMedia("(prefers-color-scheme: dark)").matches;
		const body = document.body;
		// If theme-dark or theme-light is already applied, do nothing and lock it
		if (body.classList.contains("theme-dark") || body.classList.contains("theme-light")) {
			// Set the data-color-scheme attribute based on the existing theme
			body.setAttribute('data-color-scheme', body.classList.contains("theme-dark") ? "dark" : "light");
			// Prevent future modifications
			window.__disableColorScheme = true;
			// Hide the color picker immediately
			const style = document.createElement('style');
			style.textContent = '.color-scheme-toggle { display: none !important; }';
			document.head.appendChild(style);
			return;
		}
		// Otherwise, set theme normally based on user settings or system preference
		if (window.Settings && window.Settings.preferredColorScheme) {
			const colorTheme = (window.Settings.preferredColorScheme === 'auto') && !prefersDark ? 'light'
				: (window.Settings.preferredColorScheme === 'light') ? 'light' : 'dark';
			body.setAttribute('data-color-scheme', colorTheme);
			colorTheme === "dark" ? body.classList.add("theme-dark") : body.classList.add("theme-light");
		} else {
			const colorTheme = prefersDark ? 'dark' : 'light';
			body.setAttribute('data-color-scheme', colorTheme);
			prefersDark ? body.classList.add("theme-dark") : body.classList.add("theme-light");
		}
	})();

	Manage your App Store assets
	Overview
	Your App Store images and videos help people quickly understand what your app or game offers, whether they're discovering it for the first time or coming back to see what's new.
	Use Asset Library to submit and manage your app's images and videos across platforms, sizes, and placements, including app previews, screenshots, and custom creative assets like the product page header and in-app event cards.
	App previews and screenshots. Upload app previews and screenshots to showcase your app or game in action. Set a visually compelling poster frame from your footage to highlight the moment that best conveys the essence of your app. You can also add app previews and screenshots for other devices and localizations, or archive ones you no longer need.
	Creative assets. Upload, submit, or re-use creative assets to highlight your overarching brand or value proposition across the App Store, in placements like product page header, search results, and in-app events.
	Required role: Account Holder, Admin, App Manager, or Marketing. View role permissions.
	
	
		Manage your image and video assets in Asset Library
		Use Asset Library to manage your app's images and videos, including app previews, screenshots, and creative assets. You can upload and submit creative assets on their own, independent of an app version, without specifying where you plan to use them. This lets you get them approved in advance and ready to use whenever you need them. Use your approved creative assets across eligible touch points on the App Store to drive engagement and discovery.
		Because your screenshots and app previews also live in Asset Library, you can quickly reuse existing ones, for example when creating a Custom Product Page, without having to track them down again.
		Learn more about App Store asset best practices and resources.
		Learn how to upload and submit assets to the Asset Library using the App Store Connect API.
		
			Upload assets to Asset Library
			
				You can upload and submit images and videos on their own, independent of an app version, without specifying where you plan to use them. This lets you get assets approved in advance and ready to use whenever you need them.
				Learn about app preview, screenshot, and creative asset specifications.

		Manage creative assets
		Creative assets are rich images or videos that showcase your app's or game's overarching value, brand, or excitement, distinct from the in-use functionality requirement in app previews and screenshots. You can now go beyond showing your apps and games in use, highlighting things like your brand, seasonal offerings, exciting new content, and more. In addition to standard screenshots and previews, these assets can be used to highlight a brand, promote seasonal offerings, or showcase new content.
		You can designate a creative asset to appear in organic placements across the App Store, including product page header, search results, and in-app events and more.
		
			Note: 
			
				
					Creative assets are optional and separate from your app's screenshots, app previews, In-App Purchase and Subscription images, and any promotional artwork requested by editorial teams.
				
				
					Your app previews, screenshots and creative assets used in product page header, search results and in your in-app events all live in Asset Library, the central place to manage your app's assets across platforms, sizes, and placements.
				
			
		
		If you upload a universal creative asset, you can use it for both your product page header and search results placement. Otherwise, select dedicated assets for each. Creative assets support both images and videos, and are available for iOS and iPadOS apps and games running iOS 27 or iPadOS 27 or later.
		Learn more about App Store asset best practices and resources.
		Learn about creative assets specifications.
		
			Upload creative assets
			
				You upload your custom creative assets through the Product Page Information section on the version page, directly in Asset Library, or from Custom Product Pages, Product Page Optimization, and In-App Events, then submit them for review.
				
					Note: You can submit creative assets as a standalone submission through Asset Library. Because this submission is standalone, you can upload and get creative assets approved without updating your app's version or indicating where you plan to use them. Standalone assets are reviewed against your app's latest version.
				
				Learn about creative assets specifications.
				
					
						In Apps, select the app you want to view.
					
					
						In the sidebar, click the app version under the platform you want.
					
					
						Under the Product Page Information, click the Header and Search Results tab, then click Header or Search Results, depending on which asset you want to add.
					
					
						Drag your image or video asset into the well, or, to use an asset already in Asset Library instead, click Browse Assets.
					
					
						In the dialog that appears, select the asset you want to use on the App Store, then click Save.
						
						
						
							Tip: Select Preview to review how your new assets look on the product page and search results on the App Store. You can view them for both iPhone and iPad, across different orientations and languages.
						
						
					
					
						Once everything looks good, submit your version for review as usual. After your version is approved and released, the creative assets on your product page header and in search results also go live on the App Store.
						
							Note: To use the same asset for both your product page and search results, select Use header asset in search results. This option is only available for universal creative assets. Otherwise, upload or choose a dedicated asset for search results.
						
					
				
				Learn how to upload creative assets in Asset Library.
				Learn how to submit assets from Asset Library.
			
		
		
			Update creative assets for a version already on the App Store
			
				If your app's current version is already live on the App Store, you can update its creative assets with approved assets without submitting a new version.
				
					
						In Apps, select the app you want to view.
					
					
						In the sidebar, click the version that's currently live on the App Store.
					
					
						Under the Product Page Information, click the Header and Search Results tab, then click Header or Search Results, depending on which asset you want to update.
					
					
						Click Browse Assets. In the dialog that appears, choose an approved asset from Asset Library, then click Next.
					
					
						In the dialog that appears, click Publish. Your app's product page updates with the new asset on the App Store.
						
					
				
			
		
		
			Remove creative assets from a placement
			
				
					
						In Apps, click the app you want to view.
					
					
						In the sidebar, click the app version under the platform you want.
					
					
						Click the Header and Search Results tab, then click Header or Search Results depending on which asset you want to remove.
					
					
						Click Delete.
					
				
				The asset is removed from its placement on the App Store, but isn't deleted from Asset Library. Learn how to delete or archive creative assets directly through Asset Library.
